Claims Reporting and Processing Procedure

I. Initiate Your Claim

Contact: Report your incident immediately via our online portal or by calling our 24/7 claims hotline: (800) 555-CLAIM.

Required Information: Be prepared to provide the following:

Policy Holder Name and Policy Number (e.g., AUTO-987654)

Date, Time, and Location of the Incident

Detailed Description of what happened (The Who, What, Where, When)

Contact information for any third parties or witnesses.

II. Documentation Submission

After initiating your claim (Status: SUBMITTED), you will receive a Claim ID (e.g., CLM-2025-1010). Please upload all supporting documentation within 7 business days.

Essential Documents:

Photos of the damage or incident scene.

Police or incident reports, if applicable.

Itemized repair estimates or replacement quotes.

Medical records/bills (for injury claims).

III. Review and Assessment

Status: PROCESSING
A dedicated Claims Adjuster will be assigned to your case. They will review all documentation, verify coverage against your policy terms, and may schedule a physical inspection if necessary.

Request for Additional Information (Status: ADDL_INFO_REQ):
If your adjuster requires more details, you will be notified via email and your online portal. Prompt responses are critical to avoid delays.

IV. Final Decision and Settlement

The adjuster will issue a final determination:

APPROVED: Your claim meets the coverage criteria. A settlement offer will be issued, or payment will be made directly to the service provider. (Status: APPROVED/PAID)

DENIED: The incident or loss is not covered under the terms and exclusions of your policy (e.g., policy lapsed, event is an exclusion like 'flood'). A detailed letter explaining the decision and appeal process will be provided. (Status: DENIED)
