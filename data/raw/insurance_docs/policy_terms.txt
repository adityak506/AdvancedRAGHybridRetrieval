Standard Policy Terms and Definitions

This document provides simplified definitions for key terms used in your insurance contract. For full legal definitions, refer to the Schedule and Declarations Page of your specific policy.

Term

Definition

Policyholder

The person or entity legally covered by this insurance contract.

Premium

The required monthly or annual payment made by the Policyholder to maintain coverage.

Effective Date

The date and time when coverage under the policy officially begins.

Deductible

The specific amount of money you must pay out-of-pocket before the insurer begins to cover the remainder of a loss. (E.g., if you have a $1,000 deductible, the first $1,000 of damage is your responsibility).

Claim

A formal request made by the Policyholder to the insurance company for payment based on the terms of the policy.

Coverage Limit

The maximum amount the insurance company will pay for a covered loss, as stated in the policy schedule.

Exclusion

A specific event, property, or type of loss that is not covered by the insurance policy. Common exclusions include acts of war or inherent property defects.

Actual Cash Value (ACV)

The cost to replace an item minus depreciation. This is typically less than the Replacement Cost.

Replacement Cost

The cost to replace a damaged item with a brand new one of similar kind and quality, without deduction for depreciation.
